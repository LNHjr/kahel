What you can do in Kahel

Read it the way that suits you: the plain version for business owners, or the detail your accountant will ask about.

Record sales and expenses

Type in a sale or a bill once. Kahel works out the VAT, keeps track of who owes you and whom you owe, and every report updates.

Compound lines with a VAT split per line, withholding received and deducted, multi-invoice settlements, and a customer and supplier directory with TINs for the BIR lists. Every document posts a balanced double-entry journal entry.

Run payroll

Pay your team monthly or twice a month with SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG and withholding tax already computed, plus payslips and 13th month pay.

Enrolment switches for employees not yet registered, payroll history per employee, 13th month with partial payments, year-end annualization (refund or collect), 2316 worksheets and the 1604-C alphalist CSV.

Stay ahead of the BIR

See what's due and when. Mark a return as filed with the payment details, and Kahel records the payment in your books.

Fiscal-year-aware BIR, SEC and statutory calendar; income tax estimates (graduated, 8%, RCIT/MCIT); 2307 tracking where only received certificates count as credits; BIR working papers #10 to #18 in CSV, XLSX and print.

Questions business owners ask us

Do I need to know accounting to use Kahel?

No. You record what happened (a sale, a bill, a payment) and Kahel makes the journal entries. Plain-language mode swaps terms like "accounts receivable" for "money you're owed". Your accountant can still see the full detail.

Does Kahel file my returns with the BIR?

Kahel prepares the figures, worksheets and lists and tells you when each return is due. You or your accountant still file and pay through the BIR's own channels, then mark the return as filed in Kahel.

Can my accountant or bookkeeper use it with me?

Yes. Invite them by email and choose their role. Each person who signs in uses one user seat on your plan.

We use a fiscal year, not January to December. Is that okay?

Yes. Set your fiscal year during setup and the filing calendar, reports and financial statements follow it.

We're a corporation, not a sole proprietor. Does Kahel fit?

Kahel supports sole proprietorships, one-person corporations and corporations, VAT and non-VAT, service or retail and food. Income tax estimates cover graduated rates, the 8% option and corporate RCIT and MCIT.

How do I get access and pay?

Tap Get access and our Telegram bot sends the payment details right away. Pay by GCash, Maya or bank transfer, send the screenshot in the same chat, and we set up your account. You get an Acknowledgement Receipt by email, and annual plans get a reminder before each renewal.

What happens to my data if I stop subscribing?

The business owner and the accountant can still sign in to a "Your data" screen during a retention period and download all your records. We email notices 30, 7 and 1 day before anything is deleted.
