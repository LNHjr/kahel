Privacy notice for this website

This notice covers this website and our Telegram chat. It is being reviewed by our legal counsel and may be updated. The privacy terms for using the Kahel app are shown when you first sign in.

This website

This website has no sign-up form and does not ask for your personal details. Our web host may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses and browser types, to keep the site running and secure.

When you message us on Telegram

When you tap Get access and start a chat with the Kahel bot, we receive your Telegram name and username and the messages and files you send us, such as payment screenshots, your business name and your email address.

Why we use it

  • To send you payment details and confirm your payment
  • To set up your Kahel account and email your sign-in link
  • To answer your questions and give support

We do not sell your details or use them for unrelated advertising.

Where it is kept and who sees it

Your messages stay in Telegram. The Kahel bot passes them to our team's private Telegram chat so we can reply. Only Kahel staff handling sales, onboarding and support can see them.

How long we keep it

If you become a customer, the details you gave us become part of your account records. If not, we delete our copy of your chat within 12 months of your last message, or sooner if you ask.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal details you sent us, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Message us on Telegram to ask.

You can also contact the National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph.
